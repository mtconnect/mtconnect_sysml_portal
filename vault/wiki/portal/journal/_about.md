# Journal

> **Pattern: dated milestone entries** under the parent journal-driven pattern declared in [`../_about.md`](../_about.md).

Each file follows the template at [`../../../_templates/journal-entry.md`](../../../_templates/journal-entry.md). Filenames are `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`. The timeline table is in [`../index.md`](../index.md).

Once written, journal entries are **not edited** to reflect later developments. They capture what was known and decided at the time. If a later entry supersedes a decision, link forward from the original.
